epic-patient-fhir: Terms of Use and Privacy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What this is

epic-patient-fhir is free, open-source software that lets you, or an AI assistant you choose to connect, read and download your own medical record from health systems that use Epic (MyChart). It uses the patient access API that health systems are required to provide. Source code: github.com/danhicks853/epic-patient-fhir.

Where your data goes

  • The software runs on your own computer. There is no server, account, or cloud service operated by this project.
  • Your records travel directly from your health system to your computer. They are never sent to this project.
  • You sign in on your health system's own MyChart page. The software never sees your MyChart password.
  • The access token your health system issues is held in memory for one session and is not written to disk.
  • The project collects no analytics, telemetry, or personal information.
  • If you use the software with an AI assistant (for example, Claude), the information the assistant retrieves during your conversation is sent to that assistant's provider and handled under their terms and privacy policy. Only what the assistant requests is sent, not your whole record. Using the command line export alone sends nothing anywhere.
  • The sign-in return page on this website forwards the one-time sign-in code to the software on your computer. It does not store, log, or transmit that code anywhere else, and the code cannot be used without a secret that never leaves your computer.

Access is read-only

The software only reads your record. It cannot change, add to, or delete anything in your health system's records.

Your responsibilities

  • Use the software only to access your own records, or records you are legally authorized to access.
  • Once downloaded, your records are your responsibility. Keep them secure, and be careful where you share or upload them.
  • You can revoke the software's access at any time through your MyChart account settings.

Not medical advice

This software retrieves and organizes records. It does not interpret them or provide medical advice. Talk to your care team about your health and about anything in your records.
